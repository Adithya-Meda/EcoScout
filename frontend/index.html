Material Guide

🧴

Plastics (#1 & #2)

PET & HDPE bottles are widely recyclable. Rinse clean and remove caps.

📦

Paper & Cardboard

Flatten boxes. Must be dry and completely free of food or grease stains.

🍾

Glass Containers

100% recyclable infinitely. Rinse jars and separate by color where required.

🥫

Metals & Cans

Aluminum and steel cans save massive energy. Rinse and flatten if possible.

🔋

E-Waste & Hazard

Never place in household bins! Requires municipal hazardous waste collection.

Recycling Myths vs Facts

Myth #1

"If it has a recycling symbol, it can go in any recycling bin."

Hover / Tap to Reveal Fact 💡
Verified Fact

Chasing arrows identify resin type, not local facility capability. Always check city rules.

Myth #2

"Items don't need to be rinsed before recycling."

Hover / Tap to Reveal Fact 💡
Verified Fact

Food residue contaminates entire recycling batches, causing whole loads to end up in landfills.

Myth #3

"Paper takeaway cups go in regular paper recycling."

Hover / Tap to Reveal Fact 💡
Verified Fact

Most takeaway cups have a polyethylene plastic lining that prevents paper repulping.

Our mission

Recycling rules are confusing and vary from city to city. EcoScout removes the guesswork — giving every household access to instant, accurate, and locally relevant recycling guidance. We believe small everyday decisions, made by millions of people, add up to a meaningful impact on our planet.

How it works

  1. Take a photo of any item you are unsure about — a bottle, a bag, a box, or anything else.
  2. Enter your city and PIN/ZIP code so we can look up the rules that apply specifically to where you live.
  3. Get instant advice — we tell you whether to recycle, compost, or bin it, and exactly how to prepare it.

Your privacy

We take your privacy seriously. Photos you upload are used solely to identify the item in the image — they are never stored permanently, never shared with third parties, and automatically deleted the moment your result is ready.

We do not create user accounts, we do not require login, we do not track your GPS location beyond the city and PIN/ZIP code you provide, and we do not use your data for advertising.
